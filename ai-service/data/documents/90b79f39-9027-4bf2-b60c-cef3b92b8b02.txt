Company Password Reset Procedure

Employees can reset their company password using the internal
password portal.

Step 1:
Open the internal password portal.

Step 2:
Enter your employee ID.

Step 3:
Complete identity verification.

Step 4:
Enter your new password.

Step 5:
Confirm the new password.

Step 6:
Sign in again using the new password.

If the password reset fails, contact the IT support team.
